Our organisation

History & Identity of ICOFADARE

Understanding our roots and our mission in the global fight against drug abuse

Global Presence

ICOFADARE is an International Non-Governmental Organisation headquartered in Yaoundé, Cameroon (Administrative District, B.P. 14228). It is recognised as a legal entity in all its host countries, including Cameroon, Switzerland and France.

Global Initiative

Founded in 2020, this initiative brings together experts from diverse fields: health, education, transport, sport and civil society. It continuously integrates governmental institutions and enterprises to combat this global scourge. Registration: M012618349708E.

UN SDG Alignment

Our work aligns with the Sustainable Development Goals: SDG 3 (Good Health — target 3.5: addiction prevention and treatment), SDG 4 (Quality Education) and SDG 16 (Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions).

The challenge we address

More than 3 billion people are identified as vulnerable worldwide. Dependence — chemical or behavioural (digital addictions, gambling) — erodes economic foundations, destabilises families and compromises collective public health. ICOFADARE responds to this crisis with an integrated Three-Dimensional Mechanism: Prevention, Management and Rehabilitation.

Our Vision

"To lead a global transition towards a substance-free civilisation, restoring human dignity through scientific intervention, multidisciplinary education, and the systemic reintegration of vulnerable people."
Operational framework

The 3D Mechanism

An integrated ecosystem covering the past, present and future of the addiction journey

Prevention

Reducing demand through education, cognitive resilience and media literacy. Primary, secondary and tertiary strategies.

Suppression

Cooperation with security services to disrupt trafficking networks and the socio-economic drivers of the drug trade.

Support & Management

Multidisciplinary Care Model (MCM): supervised medical detoxification, CBT therapy, digital detox clinics.

Rehabilitation

Socio-economic reintegration programmes, skills acquisition centres, "Clean Start" employment network and blockchain registry.

Branch mission

National Executive Council Mission — Indonesia

Localising the 3D Mechanism across the Indonesian archipelago, from Jakarta to Surabaya, to address urban and digital addictions.

Prevention

Education and awareness of drug abuse risks through school and community programmes integrated into Indonesian national curricula.

Clinical Treatment

Evidence-based treatment services: medical detoxification, CBT therapy, psychiatric management of severe addiction.

Community Support

Resources for individuals, families and affected communities: peer support circles, stigma workshops, professional reintegration.
